# 🔺 CIA Triad

> "The CIA Triad is the foundation of Cyber Security. Every security measure aims to protect one or more of these three principles."

---

# 🧩 What is the CIA Triad?

The **CIA Triad** is a security model that defines the three primary objectives of information security:

- 🔒 **Confidentiality**
- ✅ **Integrity**
- ⚡ **Availability**

These principles help organizations protect their systems and data from cyber threats.

---

# 🔒 Confidentiality

## 📖 Definition

Confidentiality ensures that **only authorized users** can access sensitive information.

### 🎯 Goal

- Prevent unauthorized access
- Protect privacy
- Keep sensitive information secret

### 🛡️ How to Achieve It

- Strong Passwords
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Encryption
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- VPN

### 💡 Examples

- Your Gmail password is only known to you.
- Only doctors can access a patient's medical records.
- Bank account details are visible only to the account holder.

### ⚠️ Threats

- Phishing
- Data Breaches
- Weak Passwords
- Insider Threats
- Spyware

---

# ✅ Integrity

## 📖 Definition

Integrity ensures that data remains **accurate, complete, and unmodified** unless changed by an authorized user.

### 🎯 Goal

- Prevent unauthorized modification
- Maintain data accuracy
- Build trust in information

### 🛡️ How to Achieve It

- Hashing
- Digital Signatures
- Checksums
- File Permissions
- Version Control

### 💡 Examples

- A student's exam marks cannot be changed without permission.
- Bank transaction records remain accurate.
- Medical prescriptions cannot be altered by unauthorized users.

### ⚠️ Threats

- Malware
- Hackers
- Human Error
- Software Bugs

---

# ⚡ Availability

## 📖 Definition

Availability ensures that systems, services, and data are **accessible whenever authorized users need them**.

### 🎯 Goal

- Keep systems online
- Minimize downtime
- Ensure business continuity

### 🛡️ How to Achieve It

- Regular Backups
- Redundant Servers
- Load Balancing
- Disaster Recovery Plans
- UPS (Power Backup)

### 💡 Examples

- Online banking should be available 24/7.
- Hospital systems must remain online during emergencies.
- Cloud storage should be accessible anytime.

### ⚠️ Threats

- DDoS Attacks
- Hardware Failure
- Power Outages
- Ransomware
- Natural Disasters

| Principle | Example |
|-----------|---------|

| ✅ Integrity | Transactions cannot be modified without authorization. |
| ⚡ Availability | Customers can access banking services anytime. |

# 🎯 Security Objectives

The CIA Triad helps achieve the following objectives:

- 🔒 Protect sensitive information
- ✅ Maintain data accuracy and integrity
- ⚡ Ensure continuous access to systems
- 🤝 Build customer trust
- 🏢 Support business continuity
- 🛡️ Reduce cyber risks
