# 🕵️ Understanding Types of Attackers

> "Not every hacker has the same goal. Some seek fame, some seek money, some seek revenge, and others act on behalf of a nation."

---

# 🧩 Who are Cyber Attackers?

A **Cyber Attacker** is an individual or group that attempts to gain unauthorized access to systems, networks, or data.

Their motives may include:

- 💰 Financial gain
- 🕵️ Espionage
- 📢 Political activism
- 😈 Revenge
- 🎯 Personal challenge
- 🌍 National interest

---

# 👶 Script Kiddies

## 📖 Definition

**Script Kiddies** are beginners who use **existing hacking tools and scripts** created by others instead of developing their own.

They usually have **limited technical knowledge**.

### 🎯 Motive

- Fun
- Curiosity
- Showing off
- Gaining attention

### 💡 Characteristics

- Little hacking knowledge
- Depend on public tools
- Target easy victims
- Often attack without understanding the consequences

### 🌍 Example

A teenager downloads a DDoS tool from the internet and attacks a gaming server.

---

# 💰 Cyber Criminals

## 📖 Definition

**Cyber Criminals** are individuals or organized groups who commit cyber attacks **for financial gain**.

### 🎯 Motive

- Steal money
- Sell stolen data
- Demand ransom
- Financial fraud

### 💡 Common Activities

- Ransomware
- Phishing
- Credit card theft
- Banking malware
- Identity theft

### 🌍 Example

A group sends fake bank emails to steal customers' login credentials.

---

# 👤 Insider Threats

## 📖 Definition

An **Insider Threat** is a trusted person inside an organization who intentionally or accidentally causes security problems.

### 🎯 Motive

- Revenge
- Financial gain
- Negligence
- Human error

### 💡 Types

### 🔴 Malicious Insider

Intentionally steals or damages company data.

### 🟡 Negligent Insider

Accidentally exposes sensitive information.

### 🌍 Example

An employee copies confidential company files before resigning.

---

# 📢 Hacktivists

## 📖 Definition

**Hacktivists** are attackers who hack systems to promote a **political, social, or ideological cause**.

### 🎯 Motive

- Protest
- Raise awareness
- Political message
- Social activism

### 💡 Common Activities

- Website defacement
- DDoS attacks
- Data leaks
- Public disclosures

### 🌍 Example

A group defaces a government website to protest a new law.

---

# 🌍 Nation-State Actors

## 📖 Definition

**Nation-State Actors** are highly skilled hackers who work for or are supported by a government.

They are among the **most advanced and dangerous** cyber attackers.

### 🎯 Motive

- Cyber espionage
- National security
- Military intelligence
- Political influence
- Critical infrastructure attacks

### 💡 Common Targets

- Government agencies
- Military organizations
- Banks
- Power grids
- Telecommunication companies

### 🌍 Example

A government-sponsored hacking group steals confidential military information from another country.

---

---

### 💰 Cyber Criminal

Deploys ransomware to encrypt a company's files and demands payment.

---

### 👤 Insider Threat

---

Defaces a government website to spread a political message.

---

### 🌍 Nation-State Actor

A government-backed group targets another country's power grid to gather intelligence.

# 💭 Easy Way to Remember

🏠 Imagine a Bank

- 👶 **Script Kiddie** → Throws stones at the bank for fun.
- 💰 **Cyber Criminal** → Robs the bank for money.
